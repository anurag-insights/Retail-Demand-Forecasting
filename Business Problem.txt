Evergreen Mart – Retail Demand Forecasting

Evergreen Mart is a retail business selling beverages and other grocery products.

The problem:

The company currently estimates product demand using previous sales records and employees' assumptions.

This creates two major problems:

1. Overstocking

The company purchases more products than customers actually need. Unsold products occupy storage space and increase inventory costs.


2. Stock-outs

The company sometimes runs out of products during weekends, promotions, or periods of higher demand, resulting in lost sales.



The business objective:

Develop a time-series forecasting system that predicts future product demand using historical sales, calendar variables, promotions, pricing, and other relevant factors.

The company wants to use these predictions to make better purchasing and inventory decisions.






How will we solve this problem?

Our project will follow this workflow:
1. Data Collection
   Obtain historical retail sales data.

2. Data Quality Assessment
   Identify missing values, incorrect entries, duplicates, inconsistent formats, and outliers.

3. Data Cleaning
   Handle missing values, correct inconsistent data, and investigate inaccurate records.

4. Exploratory Data Analysis
   Identify trends, seasonality, weekly patterns, and demand fluctuations.

5. Feature Engineering
   Create lag features, rolling averages, calendar variables, and other useful predictors.

6. Model Development
   Build and compare forecasting models.

7. Model Evaluation
   Compare MAE, RMSE, and WAPE.

8. Business Recommendations
   Translate forecasts into inventory-planning recommendations.
